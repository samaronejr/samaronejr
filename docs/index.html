03 / Approach

Good models are only part of the system.

I treat research code, evaluation design, and claim discipline as first-class engineering work—not supporting details.

  1. 01

    Evidence before claims

    Match every conclusion to the experiment, split, metric, and uncertainty that actually support it.

  2. 02

    Reproducibility by design

    Build deterministic, config-driven workflows with traceable artifacts instead of relying on hidden notebook state.

  3. 03

    Domain context matters

    Treat sensor behavior, temporal dynamics, and physical plausibility as part of the modeling problem.
